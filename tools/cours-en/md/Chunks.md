# 💬 Die 200 Chunks — The 200 most useful blocks in German

> **2 chunks a day for the first 20 days, then 1 a day until Tag 180 = 200 ready-made blocks** for speaking without translating.

## 1. Why chunks solve the translation problem

In German, translating word for word from your own language almost always gives a wrong sentence: the word order is different, the prepositions are different, and many expressions have nothing in common (*"I'm cold"* = ***Mir** ist kalt*, literally “to me is cold”).  
A native German speaker doesn't build these sentences: they **pull ready-made blocks** out of memory. *Wie geht's?*, *Es kommt darauf an.*, *Ich freue mich auf…* are single “words” to them.  
German bonus: a chunk contains **grammar that is already correct** (the right case, the verb in the right place). By learning *Ich habe Lust auf Pizza*, you learn without thinking about it that *auf* is followed by the accusative.

## 2. How to learn them

**1.** **Every day**, the *💬 Chunk(s) des Tages* block in your `DXXX` file gives you 1 or 2 new chunks (5 min).  
**2.** **Out loud, in one breath**, with the German melody. Listen to them on **Forvo** or **YouGlish** (choose *German*).  
**3.** **One true sentence about your life** with each one — that's what makes them stick.  
**4.** **Quick recall**: every day, you say again the chunks from 7 days ago and 30 days ago (listed in the day's file).  
**5.** **Anki**: the 200 chunks are in the sub-deck `Deutsch B2::3 Chunks` of `Deutsch_B2.apkg` (front = the gapped sentence, back = the chunk).  
**6.** **Chunk hunting**: during Hören (Easy German, Nicos Weg…), write down the blocks that come up often in section 6.

**Self-test**: hide the *Chunk* column of a category, read the English or the example, and say the chunk out loud. Goal: answer in **under 2 seconds**.

## 5. The traps of word-for-word translation

These mistakes all come from the same reflex: translating from your own language. Learn the right-hand version as one block.

| English | ❌ Word for word | ✅ The German chunk |
|---|---|---|
| I'm 25 (French: “I have 25 years”) | ~~Ich habe 25 Jahre~~ | **Ich bin 25 (Jahre alt)** |
| I'm fine (French: “I go well”) | ~~Ich gehe gut~~ | **Mir geht's gut** |
| I'm cold | ~~Ich bin kalt~~ | **Mir ist kalt** |
| I like it | ~~Ich gefalle das~~ | **Das gefällt mir** |
| It depends | ~~Es hängt~~ | **Es kommt darauf an** |
| I've lived here for 3 years | ~~Ich habe hier seit 3 Jahren gewohnt~~ | **Ich wohne seit 3 Jahren hier** |
| I'm waiting for the bus | ~~Ich warte für den Bus~~ | **Ich warte auf den Bus** |
| The weather is nice | ~~Es macht schön~~ | **Es ist schön / Das Wetter ist schön** |
| To make a decision | ~~eine Entscheidung machen~~ | **eine Entscheidung treffen** |
| To take a break | ~~eine Pause nehmen~~ | **eine Pause machen** |
| To be right | ~~Recht sein~~ | **recht haben** |
| I'm working (right now) | ~~Ich bin arbeitend~~ | **Ich arbeite gerade** |
