# 🇩🇪 Deutsch Intensiv — From zero to B2 in 6 months (180 days)

> An intensive programme of **180 days × 4 h** to go from **zero** to **B2 level**, pass the **Goethe-Zertifikat B2** (or telc/ÖSD B2) and handle **job interviews in German** — especially for the campuses of the **42 network** in Germany, Austria and Switzerland (Berlin, Wolfsburg, Heilbronn, Wien…).

---

## 1. Is it realistic? (yes, but only if you're consistent)

- 180 days × 4 h = **720 hours**. The usual estimates for reaching B2 from zero are in this range for a motivated learner putting in steady independent work.
- It works **on one condition**: 4 hours **every day**, Anki **even on rest days**, and speaking **from day one**.
- You have an advantage: English and German are close cousins. Lots of German words look like English (*Haus/house, Wasser/water, kommen/come*), and if you can learn on your own (42!), you already know how to work with this method.
- If you fall behind: **shift, don't skip**. Better to finish on D195 with solid foundations than on D180 with gaps.

## 4. The “3 Verben des Tages”

German's answer to English *phrasal verbs*: **verbs**, their **irregular forms** (Präsens, Präteritum, Perfekt), **separable verbs** and **verbs with prepositions**. The programme contains **180 essential verbs**, introduced at the pace of your level (A1 verbs at the start, B2 verbs at the end):
- **Entdecken** (one day in three): 3 new verbs to discover and use.
- **Wiederholen** (~9 days later): explain them from memory, use them in your text.
- **Aktivieren** (~30 days later): a one-minute mini-story using all three.

**The chunks.** These are ready-made blocks (*Es kommt darauf an*, *Ich habe Lust auf…*, *Mir ist kalt*) with the grammar already correct. There are 2 chunks a day from Tag 1 to Tag 20 (survival expressions), then 1 a day until Tag 180, so 200 in total. Every day you also say again the ones from 7 and 30 days earlier. The full list, the word-for-word translation traps and the method are in `Chunks`.

**The Geschichte des Tages.** Every day ends with an episode of the story of **Malik**, the hero of the English story, who arrives in Berlin to work at 42 Berlin and learns German from zero, like you. Each episode reuses **the grammar, vocabulary, Verben and chunks of the day** (in bold). The length grows with your level: about 250 words at A1, with a **Hilfe** line translating the words you don't know yet, then about 300 at A2 and 350 at B1–B2. Read it, read it again out loud, then summarise it or retell it in German.

## 5. The method (German special)

1. **Never a noun without its article and plural**: *der Tisch, die Tische*. In Anki, one colour per gender (🔵 der, 🔴 die, 🟢 das).
2. **Two golden rules of word order**: the **conjugated verb in position 2** in the main clause; the **verb at the end** in the subordinate clause. They come up all the time — they must become a reflex.
3. **Don't translate, build blocks**: learn *ich habe keine Zeit*, *es kommt darauf an*, *Ich freue mich auf…* as units.
4. **Listen a huge amount**: 45 min a day minimum, first adapted (Nicos Weg, Easy German, Slow German), then authentic (Tagesschau, Deutschlandfunk). **German** subtitles, never English.
5. **Speak from D1**: even 4 sentences. Record yourself every day.
6. **Simplify**: if a sentence is too complicated, say a simpler one. *Ich habe keine Ahnung* is better than a long silence.
7. **Explain instead of searching**: *„Das ist ein Ding, mit dem man…"*, *„eine Person, die…"* (relative clauses, D48).
8. **Gradual switch to German**: explanations in your language up to A2, then in German (B1–B2). That's on purpose: you learn German **in German**.

## 6. Which exam? (register around D120–123)

| Exam | Format | Points forts | Good to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Goethe-Zertifikat B2** ⭐ *recommended* | 4 modules: Lesen (65 min), Hören (40 min), Schreiben (75 min), Sprechen (~15 min, in pairs) | The best known worldwide, **modular** (you can retake a single module), Goethe centres in many countries | The programme follows this format |
| **telc Deutsch B2** | Written + oral, slightly different format | Very widespread in Germany (employers, authorities), often cheaper | Free sample papers on telc.net |
| **ÖSD Zertifikat B2** | Format close to the Goethe | The reference in **Austria** (useful if you're aiming for 42 Wien) | Sample papers on osd.at |

💶 Expect around **€250–350** (or the local equivalent) for the full Goethe B2, depending on the centre — **check** on goethe.de or on the telc/ÖSD centre's website.
📅 Registration often closes **4–6 weeks before**: aim for a session around **D180–190** and register around **D120–123** (the programme reminds you).
🆓 **Free sample exams**: goethe.de → *Prüfungen* → *Goethe-Zertifikat A1/A2/B1/B2* → *Übungsmaterialien* (one *Modellsatz* per level) · telc.net · osd.at. For the 3 B2 mock exams, an official practice book is useful (e.g. *Mit Erfolg zum Goethe-Zertifikat B2*, *Fit fürs Goethe-Zertifikat B2*).
